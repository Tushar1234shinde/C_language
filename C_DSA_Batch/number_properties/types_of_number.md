# Most Asked Number Properties

This file covers **the most frequently asked number properties** in **college exams, practicals, viva, and technical interviews**. Each property includes definition, key logic, and why it is important.

---

## 1. Prime Number

**Definition:** A number having exactly two divisors: 1 and itself.

**Examples:** 2, 3, 5, 7, 11

**Core Logic:**

* Check divisibility from 2 to n/2 (or √n for optimization)

**Why Asked:**

* Fundamental logic building
* Used in cryptography and DSA

---

## 2. Palindrome Number

**Definition:** A number that reads the same forward and backward.

**Examples:** 121, 1331, 999

**Core Logic:**

* Reverse the number and compare with original

**Why Asked:**

* Tests digit extraction
* Common in viva and coding rounds

---

## 3. Armstrong Number

**Definition:** Sum of each digit raised to the power of number of digits equals the number.

**Example:**
153 → 1³ + 5³ + 3³ = 153

**Core Logic:**

* Count digits
* Extract digits
* Apply power and sum

**Why Asked:**

* Very popular university question
* Tests loops + math

---

## 4. Perfect Number

**Definition:** Sum of proper divisors equals the number.

**Example:**
6 → 1 + 2 + 3 = 6

**Core Logic:**

* Find divisors excluding the number
* Calculate sum

**Why Asked:**

* Divisor logic
* Frequently in theory + practical exams

---

## 5. Strong Number

**Definition:** Sum of factorial of digits equals the number.

**Example:**
145 → 1! + 4! + 5! = 145

**Core Logic:**

* Digit extraction
* Factorial calculation

**Why Asked:**

* Combination of loops and functions

---

## 6. Neon Number

**Definition:** Sum of digits of square of the number equals the number.

**Example:**
9 → 9² = 81 → 8 + 1 = 9

**Why Asked:**

* Short logic question
* Common in MCQs

---

## 7. Automorphic Number

**Definition:** A number whose square ends with the same number.

**Example:**
25 → 625

**Why Asked:**

* String/number comparison logic

---

## 8. Harshad (Niven) Number

**Definition:** A number divisible by the sum of its digits.

**Example:**
18 → 1 + 8 = 9, 18 % 9 = 0

**Why Asked:**

* Simple but tricky
* Good interview warm-up

---

## 9. Fibonacci Property

**Definition:** Each term is the sum of previous two terms.

**Example:**
0 1 1 2 3 5 8

**Why Asked:**

* Extremely common
* Loop + variable update logic

---

## 10. Even & Odd Numbers

**Definition:**

* Even → divisible by 2
* Odd → not divisible by 2

**Why Asked:**

* Basic screening question
* Used inside many other problems

---

## 11. Spy Number

**Definition:** Sum of digits equals product of digits.

**Example:**
112 → sum = 4, product = 4

**Why Asked:**

* Less common but impressive if known

---

## IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE (Exam Favorite)

**Number Series vs Number Properties**

* Series → generate sequence
* Properties → check a number
